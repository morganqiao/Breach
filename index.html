📖 HOW TO PLAY

🖱️

1. DRAG TO CONNECT

Click and drag across adjacent nodes to form a chain. The longer the chain, the more credits you earn!

🎯

2. RELEASE TO EXTRACT

Release with 4+ nodes connected to extract data. Fewer than 4 will damage your stability!

❤️

3. SPECIAL RED NODES

Red nodes are worth 2x credits! Look for them and include them in your chains.

⚡

4. SKILLS & POWER-UPS

Use skills from the bottom panel. Collect power-ups that appear when you extract data.

👾

5. AVOID ENEMIES

Firewalls (red), Viruses (purple), and Trackers (gold) will damage you. Touch them to fight back!

👑

6. BOSS FIGHTS

Every 50 sectors, a boss appears. Defeat it by connecting nodes near enemies. Win 1000 CR!
